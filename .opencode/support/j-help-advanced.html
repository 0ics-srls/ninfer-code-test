1. Feature Development Flow

graph TD
    A["You have an idea"] --> B["j-new-feature"]
    B --> C["Tell me what you need\n(I listen, you talk)"]
    C --> D["Say 'done'"]
    D --> E["I analyze the code\nand search for\nexisting solutions"]
    E --> F["I propose a solution\nYou approve or adjust"]
    F --> G["I create a\nstep-by-step plan"]
    G --> H{"Plan OK?"}
    H -->|"Change it"| F
    H -->|"Looks good!"| I["j-develop"]
    I --> J["I build it\nstep by step"]
    J --> K["All done, tests pass"]
    K --> L["j-close"]
    L --> M["Merged and deployed!"]

2. Bug Fix Flow

graph TD
    A["Something is broken!"] --> B["j-bug"]
    B --> C["Tell me:\n- What should happen?\n- What happens instead?\n- Where?"]
    C --> D["Say 'done'"]
    D --> E["I investigate\nthe problem"]
    E --> F["I explain what's wrong\nand propose a fix"]
    F --> G{"Fix OK?"}
    G -->|"Try different"| E
    G -->|"Go ahead"| H["I fix it and\nrun tests"]
    H --> I{"Tests pass?"}
    I -->|"No"| E
    I -->|"Yes"| J["j-close"]
    J --> K["Fix merged\nand deployed!"]

    style A fill:#f44336,color:#fff
    style K fill:#4CAF50,color:#fff
    style H fill:#2196F3,color:#fff
    style J fill:#FF9800,color:#fff

4. Session Lifecycle

graph TD
    A["Start session"] --> B{"First time\non this project?"}
    B -->|"Yes"| C["j-setup"]
    B -->|"No"| D{"Continuing\nprevious work?"}
    C --> D
    D -->|"Yes"| E["j-continue\nResumes where\nyou left off"]
    D -->|"No"| F{"What to do?"}
    E --> G["Keep working..."]
    F -->|"New feature"| H["j-new-feature"]
    F -->|"Fix bug"| I["j-bug"]
    F -->|"Experiment"| J["j-poc"]
    H --> G
    I --> G
    J --> G
    G --> K{"Done for today?"}
    K -->|"Yes"| L["j-save\nEverything saved!"]
    K -->|"Feature complete"| M["j-close\nMerge and deploy"]
